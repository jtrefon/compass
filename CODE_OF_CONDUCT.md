# Code of Conduct

Compass is built in the open, by people who care about the craft. The whole policy fits in one
sentence:

**Treat other contributors the way you'd want to be treated.**

That means:

- Be constructive. Critique code, not people.
- Assume good faith. We're all here to make a better editor, not to win arguments.
- Credit generously, complain privately, fix publicly.
- No harassment, no gatekeeping, no personal attacks. Not in issues, not in PRs, not in
  Discussions, not in the Discord that will inevitably exist.
- Respect that this is a solo-maintainer project with a day job and a life. Maintainers answer
  when they can, not on demand.

## Reporting

If you see a violation, open an issue or email <jack@trefon.com>. Reports are handled privately,
and we act: a warning first, removal from the project if it continues.

## Scope

This applies to every space associated with the Compass project — GitHub, the website, and any
future community channels. It applies to maintainers too. Nobody is exempt.

— Jacek, maintainer
