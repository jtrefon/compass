Features

Everything you need. Nothing you tolerate.

The AI editor you actually wanted: agentic power on your terms, private intelligence on your machine, and performance that feels native — because it is.

Real AI, on your machine

Completion that's actually intelligent.

Ghost-text completion in <100ms from a 4B on-device model — genuine intelligence, not a fake heuristic. Retrieval runs on the Apple Neural Engine, so indexing and search stay silent and painless while you keep typing.

Offline? Still works. Plane, tunnel, coffee shop — your AI doesn't quit because it never needed the network.

Honest about what it isn't

What Compass is not

Compass is not a Linux editor, not a Windows editor, and not an enterprise procurement product — not yet, anyway. It's not a cloud-only SaaS, and it's not a browser pretending to be an app. We'd rather be the best native AI IDE for macOS than a mediocre one everywhere. If that's not your setup, no hard feelings — come back when Apple Silicon is.

Try it. It's free — really.

MIT open source. No account, no trial, no credit card. Requires Apple Silicon and macOS 26+.
